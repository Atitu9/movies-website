Behind the website

About MovieTime

A small student project about something we like talking about: movies.

The idea

Choosing a film can take longer than expected. MovieTime keeps the list small: a few different genres, a short description of each film and enough information to decide what fits your evening.

This website was made as a frontend midterm project. The collection includes science fiction, animation, action and comedy, with a separate page for longer reviews.

How the collection is organised

  1. Browse the posters or choose a genre in the collection.
  2. Open a synopsis to get an idea of the story.
  3. Check the running time before you pick a film.
  4. Read a review or suggest another movie.

Quick facts

MovieTime is a guide to films. To watch a movie, check the legal streaming or rental services available in your country.

A few questions

Can I watch movies here?

No. MovieTime provides descriptions and reviews, not movie streams or downloads.

Are the reviews spoiler-free?

They discuss the premise, style and general impressions without explaining the endings.

Can I suggest a movie?

Yes. Visit the contact page to learn how to reach the project.
