A closer look

Movie reviews

Short, spoiler-free thoughts on three films from the collection. These are opinions, so your favourites may be different.

2014 · Sci-Fi · Christopher Nolan

Interstellar

The best part of Interstellar is the way a huge space journey stays connected to a small family. The quiet moments between Cooper and his daughter matter as much as the planets and spacecraft.

Hans Zimmer's music gives the film a lot of its energy. Some explanations are long, and the running time will not suit every evening, but it is a rewarding choice when you can give it your full attention.

Best for: a long movie night with the sound turned up.

2001 · Animation · Hayao Miyazaki

Spirited Away

The bathhouse feels like a place that keeps running even when Chihiro is not on screen. Small details in the backgrounds make the world interesting to explore, while Chihiro's gradual confidence gives the story a clear centre.

Not every creature or rule is explained. That can feel strange at first, but it also makes the film feel like a dream. The slower scenes are some of its most memorable moments.

Best for: getting lost in an imaginative animated world.

2007 · Animation · Brad Bird

Ratatouille

A rat who wants to cook is a funny idea, and the film makes it work through the busy kitchen scenes and Remy's partnership with Linguini. The food looks so good that watching on an empty stomach is probably a mistake.

Its message about talent and opportunity is easy to understand without making the characters perfect. It is a warm, funny film that is also worth revisiting for its final restaurant scene.

Best for: an easy evening with friends or family.
